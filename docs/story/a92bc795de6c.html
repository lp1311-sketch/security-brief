FBI says SafePay ransomware gang ‘among most active’ with more than 600 victims since 2024

Published: 20 Oct 2026, 20:27 UTC · Priority score 6

Summary

The FBI and cyber agencies in the UK and Germany said SafePay is targeting businesses and critical infrastructure by guessing passwords, exploiting vulnerabilities and using phishing attacks.

Summary is the feed's excerpt, shortened. It is not a full article.

Why it matters

Ransomware intrusions commonly start with stolen credentials, exposed remote-access services or phishing. Look for new admin accounts, unusual logons and mass file changes in the affected period. Exposed keys or passwords stay valid until rotated. Rotate the affected keys and check their usage logs for unexpected calls.
